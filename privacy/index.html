// PRIVACY

What we keep.

Short version: one email address, to send you a download link. Nothing that follows you around.

The PanUrgos Studio app

The app sends us nothing. It has no telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking. Your code, prompts and models stay on your machine. Anything the app does reach out for (model downloads from Hugging Face, llama.cpp updates from GitHub) is off until you allow it and is listed in the app's Network Ledger.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no ad pixels, and nothing loaded from other companies. Fonts and images are served from panurgos.com itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, handles the connection and may keep standard server logs; we don't receive or use them.

When you download

To download PanUrgos Studio you give us an email address, either by typing it or by signing in with Google or GitHub. We store:

  • your email address,
  • the platform you chose (Windows, macOS or Linux),
  • whether you asked to hear about new versions,
  • how you signed in (email, Google or GitHub),
  • when you signed up, verified and downloaded, and which version.

We don't store your IP address, device details or anything about your browsing. Download links are single-use and expire after 30 minutes; we keep only a scrambled (hashed) form of them and delete expired ones daily.

Signing in with Google or GitHub

If you choose Google or GitHub, we ask them only for your verified email address. We don't receive your password, and we don't ask for your name, photo, contacts, repositories or anything else. We use their access token once to read the email address, then discard it; we never store it.

Why

To make sure each download goes to a real person, to count how many people use PanUrgos, and to tell you when a new version or your platform ships, only if you ticked that box. We never sell, rent or share your email, and we never use it for advertising.

Who handles it

  • Cloudflare runs the download service and stores the data above.
  • Resend delivers the emails with your download link.
  • Google or GitHub, only if you choose to sign in with them, confirm your email address.

Both act only on our instructions, for these purposes.

Deleting your data

Ask and we'll delete everything we hold about you. Email us.Contact us through the PanUrgos GitHub page. We keep your data until you ask us to delete it, or for two years after your last download, whichever comes first.
