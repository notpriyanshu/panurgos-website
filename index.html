// INQUIRIES

Questions, answered.

Does any of my data leave my computer?

Not when you use a local model, which is the default. The model runs in llama.cpp on 127.0.0.1, and the editor interface can't reach the network at all. If you choose to add a cloud provider, only what you send it goes out, and each request is listed in the Network Ledger.

What is PanUrgos Sovereign?

The organisation platform we're building next: your models on your own servers, reachable only by employees and personnel you authorise, with an audit trail of every request. PanUrgos Studio, the private code editor, is available today.

What hardware do I need?

Any 64-bit Windows, macOS or Linux machine. An NVIDIA card with 6 GB of memory comfortably runs 7B to 8B coding models, Apple Silicon Macs use unified memory, and smaller models still run on the CPU alone.

Which models can I run?

Any GGUF model from Hugging Face, such as Qwen Coder, DeepSeek, Gemma or Llama, plus models you already have in LM Studio. You can also connect Unsloth or any OpenAI-compatible server, including your own fine-tunes.

Is it really free?

PanUrgos Studio is MIT licensed, with no account, subscription or usage limits. Your hardware does the work, so there's nothing to meter.

Why the name?

Πανοῦργος (panourgos) is Ancient Greek for one who is ready to do anything: capable of anything. The full power of AI, working for you alone.
