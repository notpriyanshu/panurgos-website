// HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, all inside your network.

PanUrgos is installed on servers you own or that we set up for you. Everything below happens on that hardware.

01

Connect

Read-only connectors for email, documents, databases, CRM and ERP systems, ticketing, chat and file shares, plus machine and sensor data where you have it. New and changed data is picked up continuously.

02

Store

Data is indexed and encrypted on your own storage, under the retention rules you set. Nothing is copied to us or to any other vendor.

03

Learn

Open models run on your GPUs and are fine-tuned on your data, so they learn your products, terms and processes. The trained models belong to you.

04

Act

Assistants answer staff questions with sources, write reports, flag unusual activity and carry out routine tasks across your systems, with approval steps where you want them.

USUAL ENTERPRISE AI

  • Your data is sent to a provider's cloud
  • Models are rented, not owned
  • Costs grow with every request
  • Stops working when the connection does
→

PANURGOS FOR ENTERPRISES

  • Data is processed and stored on your premises
  • Models are trained on your data and stay yours
  • Runs on hardware you already paid for
  • Works without an internet connection

The cloud is optional. If you allow it, PanUrgos can fetch software updates and new open models. Each request is listed in the audit log, and none of them contain your business data.

// FAQ

Questions

Does any of our data leave our premises?

No. Data is collected, stored, processed and used for training on your own hardware. The only optional outside connections are software updates and new open models, which you can switch off entirely.

Which systems can it connect to?

Common ones such as email, file shares, document stores, databases, CRM, ERP and ticketing tools, plus custom systems through their APIs or databases. On the call we'll go through yours.

Do we need our own AI team?

No. We install it, connect your systems and set up the first models with you. Your IT team looks after the hardware like any other server.

Can it run without an internet connection?

Yes. It can run fully air-gapped. Updates can then be applied from approved offline media.

How is it priced?

Pricing is set per deployment, based on its size and the hardware involved. Early-access partners help shape it, and we'll discuss it on the call.

When can we start?

PanUrgos for Enterprises is in early access now. Book a call to talk about a pilot.
